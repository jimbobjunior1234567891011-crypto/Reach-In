SEE IT WORK

Hover the box, drag straight into the grid, shift-click straight back out. The box never leaves your inventory.

EVERYWHERE YOU'D WANT IT

Your inventory, chests, barrels, ender chests — anywhere a container screen opens.

Sorting a chest stops being a chore

Open a chest with a shulker in your bag and both are live at once. Pull from one, drop into the other, no placing the box down and breaking it again afterwards.

Coloured to match the box

The panel takes the dye colour of the shulker you're looking into, so a wall of them stays legible at a glance. Undyed boxes get their own purple.

  • Left & right clickPick up, place, split a stack in half — exactly as anywhere else.
  • Shift-clickStraight into the box from your inventory, straight back out to your hotbar.
  • Drag to distributeSpread a stack across several cells in one motion.
  • Double-click collectGather every matching item, including the ones inside the box.
  • Number keysSwap a cell straight to a hotbar slot.

None of that is reimplemented. The grid's cells are real inventory slots, so the game's own click handling runs on them unchanged — which is why they behave.

Vanilla has no packet for moving an item inside a shulker box you're carrying.

A client-only mod can show you the contents, but any move it makes is a lie the next server sync erases. So Reach-In ships on both sides and is required on both — every move is applied and validated by the server.

INSTALL

Drop the jar in the mods folder — on the client and the server.

  1. Install NeoForge 21.1.x for Minecraft 1.21.1.
  2. Put reachin-0.1.0.jar in your client's mods folder.
  3. Put the same jar in your server's mods folder. Client-only won't connect; server-only does nothing.
  4. Launch, open your inventory, hover a shulker box.

cd Reach-In
.\build.ps1

PLAYS NICE WITH SHULKER BOX TOOLTIP

While the grid is open, the shulker's own tooltip is suppressed so it can't cover the panel. That also hides Shulker Box Tooltip's preview, which draws through the same pipeline — so the two never stack on top of each other. Keep it installed or don't. Either works.
